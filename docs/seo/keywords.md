## ⚠️ FLAG: Brand vs Domain Spelling Discrepancy (fix before any SEO spend)

The brand is spelled **"Elara"** (Little **Elara** Steps) but the client-provided domain is **https://littleelerasteps.com** ("El**e**ra"). This is a hard branding/SEO problem:

- **Brand-search mismatch:** Parents who see signage, Instagram (`littleelarasteps`), or word-of-mouth will type "elara" and may not land on an "elera" domain. Branded search is the easiest traffic a local business gets — you'll leak it.
- **NAP / citation inconsistency:** Every directory, GBP, and backlink must use ONE exact business name. Two spellings split authority and confuse Google's entity matching.
- **Trust signal:** A misspelled domain vs. brand name reads as careless to parents evaluating a daycare for a vulnerable child.

**Recommended action (in priority order):**

1. **Best:** Register `littleelarasteps.com` (correct spelling) and make it the canonical/primary domain. 301-redirect `littleelerasteps.com` → the correct domain so no equity or typo-traffic is lost.
2. **If the correct domain is unavailable/taken:** Keep `littleelerasteps.com` as the live site BUT (a) lock the legal/display business name to one spelling everywhere, and (b) buy the correctly-spelled domain defensively and redirect it in.
3. **Minimum:** Decide on ONE official spelling for "the brand name" used in GBP, schema `name`, title tags, and all citations — and stay 100% consistent. Do not mix.

**Decision needed from client before GBP and citation build-out begins.** This document assumes the public brand name is **"Little Elara Steps"** (Elara spelling).

> The **primary GBP category is the #1 local ranking factor** — and a _wrong_ primary category is the #1 _negative_ factor. Get this right first.

### A. GBP Categories (India-appropriate for an inclusive daycare)

**Recommended Primary category:**

You cannot rank a single category as #1 for everything, so choose the highest-revenue intent.)_

**Recommended Secondary categories (add all that apply — Google allows up to ~9):**

- `Kindergarten` (if applicable to the 5–6 yr group / "nursery-KG")

> Do not add `School` (too broad) or unrelated categories — irrelevant categories dilute relevance. Only add categories you genuinely deliver.

### B. NAP Consistency Rules (non-negotiable)

Use **one exact, character-identical NAP** on the website, GBP, and every citation:

- **Name:** `Little Elara Steps` (no suffix like "Pvt Ltd" unless that's the legal trading name shown on signage — pick one and never vary it; do NOT keyword-stuff the GBP name e.g. "Little Elara Steps Preschool & Daycare" — that risks suspension).
- **Address:** `B-298, Plot No. 12-B, Block B, New Ashok Nagar, East Delhi, Delhi 110096` — same format, spelling, abbreviations, and pincode everywhere. Pick one form of "Block B / Block-B" and lock it.
- **Phone:** `+91 93109 82342` — same number, same format (use the local primary; if a second tracking number is ever used, never put it on GBP).
- **Rules:** (1) Match the exact address format Google validates during verification. (2) Embed the _same_ Google Maps location on the Contact page. (3) Add `LocalBusiness`/`ChildCare`/`Preschool` JSON-LD with identical NAP + `geo` coordinates (5-decimal precision) + `openingHoursSpecification`. (4) Fix the brand-vs-domain spelling issue (top of doc) before submitting any citation — two spellings = split entity.

### C. GBP Profile Completeness (do all)

- Verify the listing (postcard/video verification).
- Fill **every** field: services (list each program as a service), opening hours (incl. day-care extended hours), attributes ("Identifies as women-owned" if true, "Wheelchair accessible", "Safe for kids"), "from the business" description (~750 chars, lead with primary keyword + inclusive positioning + area).
- Add **15–25 real photos**: exterior + signage (for NAP/recognition), classrooms, play area, safety (CCTV, gates), staff, activities. Add a few short videos. Geotag where possible.
- Add **Products/Services** entries: "Play School & Preschool (2–6 yrs)", "Inclusive Learning Program (2–15 yrs)", "Day Care / Creche", "Early Intervention" — each with description + keyword.
- Turn on **messaging/WhatsApp** (parents prefer WhatsApp in Delhi) and the booking/enquiry link.

### D. GBP Post Cadence

- **Post weekly (minimum 1/week; aim 2).** A consistent ~4-8 posts/month keeps the profile "active," which correlates with engagement and visibility.
- Post mix (rotate): activity/event photos (annual day, festivals, field trips), admission-open announcements ("Admissions open for 2026–27"), parent testimonials/quotes, tips ("3 signs your toddler is ready for playschool"), program spotlights (inclusive program, early intervention), offers (sibling discount, early-bird fees).
- Always include a CTA button (Call / WhatsApp / Learn more) and the area keyword in the post text.

### E. Review-Generation Tactics (critical — beware the velocity cliff)

> Review velocity matters: **a 3-week gap with zero new reviews can cause local rankings to slip** (Sterling Sky's "18-day rule"). Aim for a _steady drip_, not a one-time batch.

- **Target a steady ~2–4 new Google reviews/month**, every month — consistency beats volume spikes.
- Create a **short Google review link** (from GBP "Get more reviews"); turn it into a QR code on a printed card at pickup/admission desk and a saved WhatsApp message.
- **Ask at peak-happiness moments:** after admission, after annual day/events, after a child's milestone, end of term. Train front-desk staff to ask in person, then send the link on WhatsApp same day.
- **Respond to 100% of reviews** within 24–48h — thank by name, reference the child's program (never share medical/special-needs details publicly). Responding is itself a ranking/trust signal.
- Encourage reviewers to mention the **service + area** naturally ("inclusive daycare in New Ashok Nagar") — keyword-rich reviews help relevance, but never script or incentivise (against Google policy; incentivised reviews risk removal).
- Seed reviews across platforms too: Facebook, JustDial, and the IG (testimonials in highlights).
