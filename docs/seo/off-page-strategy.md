## 2. Review-Generation Strategy (Google reviews = top priority)

Reviews are the highest-leverage off-page signal you control. Volume, velocity, recency, and your responses all feed Local Pack rankings AND conversion. For a daycare, a parent reading "they handled my child's meltdowns with such patience" converts better than any ad.

### Targets & cadence

- **Goal:** reach **25+ Google reviews at 4.7★+** in 90 days, then a **steady drip of 3–5 new reviews/month** (Google rewards _recency and steady velocity_, not a one-time spike — a burst of 20 reviews in one day looks manipulated).
- Prioritize **Google reviews first** (biggest impact), then Facebook recommendations, then JustDial/Sulekha/UrbanPro ratings (these also influence those platforms' lead ranking).

### How to ask parents (scripts)

- **The moment matters.** Ask right after a positive interaction: a happy pickup, a milestone ("she said her first full sentence today!"), a successful therapy goal, end of a term, or after you resolve a concern well.
- **WhatsApp template (primary channel — highest response rate in India):**
  > "Hi [Parent name] 😊 We're so glad [Child]'s settling in beautifully at Little Elara Steps! If you have 30 seconds, a quick Google review really helps other parents in New Ashok Nagar find us — especially families looking for inclusive care. Here's the direct link: [GBP review short-link]. Thank you so much! 💛"
- **In-person:** train staff to mention it warmly at pickup, then follow up on WhatsApp with the link (people forget; the link in hand is what converts).
- **Make it frictionless:** use the GBP "review short link" (get it from your Business Profile → Ask for reviews). Never make a parent search for you.
- **Segment the ask for your USP:** specifically invite parents of children with ASD/ADHD/developmental delays to mention their experience — these reviews are gold for ranking on inclusive/special-needs searches and reassure the exact families you serve.
- **Never incentivize reviews** (no discounts/gifts for reviews) — it violates Google policy and risks removal. You may _thank_ generally, not pay-per-review.

### QR code idea

- Print a **"Review us on Google" QR code** (links to the GBP review short-link) and place it:
  - On a small standee at the **reception/pickup desk**.
  - On the **monthly fee receipt / report card / parent newsletter**.
  - On a card handed out at **annual day, sports day, parent-teacher meets, admission events**.
  - In the **WhatsApp parent-group pinned message** (as a tappable link, not just QR).
- Add a second QR for **Instagram follow** beside it.

### Responding to reviews (do this for EVERY review within 24–48h)

- **Positive:** thank by name, reference a specific detail, reinforce a keyword naturally.
  > "Thank you, [Name]! We're thrilled [Child] loves our sensory play sessions. It means a lot to our team at Little Elara Steps in New Ashok Nagar. 💛"
- **Negative/critical:** respond calmly, empathetically, take it offline. Never argue or reveal child details (privacy).
  > "Thank you for sharing this — we take every concern seriously. We'd love to understand and make it right. Please reach us at +91 93109 82342 or hello@[CANONICAL-DOMAIN]. — Team Little Elara Steps"
- Responding signals an active, trustworthy business to both Google and prospective parents. **Unanswered negative reviews are the biggest avoidable conversion killer.**

## 4. Social Signals & Content Strategy

Social links are largely nofollow, but they drive **discovery, brand searches, reviews, and citations** — all of which indirectly lift local rankings. For a daycare, social proof = trust = enrollments.

### Channel roles

- **Instagram (`littleelarasteps`) — primary.** Discovery + brand-building among Delhi parents. Reels are the growth engine. Use location tags (New Ashok Nagar, Mayur Vihar) and local hashtags on every post.
- **Facebook Page — trust + reviews + older-parent/grandparent reach + local parent groups.** Cross-post from IG; actively participate in East Delhi parent/RWA groups (give value first, then it's natural to mention the school).
- **WhatsApp — conversion + retention.** Business catalog, broadcast lists for announcements, a **parent community group** (drives word-of-mouth referrals, which are your cheapest enrollments). Use WhatsApp click-to-chat links in every bio and listing.
- **YouTube (optional, later):** a 60–90s facility tour + parent-testimonial video. Embed on the website; ranks for brand searches; reusable everywhere.

### Content pillars for an inclusive daycare (rotate weekly)

1. **A day at Little Elara Steps** — real moments: circle time, sensory play, meals, nap, outdoor play (faces blurred/consented per privacy).
2. **Inclusion in action** — how shadow teachers support kids with ASD/ADHD; the sensory/quiet room; "every child learns differently." This is your differentiator AND your most shareable content.
3. **Parent education / tips** — early-intervention signs, milestone guides, school-readiness, screen-time, tantrum strategies. (Position as the _helpful expert_ → earns saves, shares, and links.)
4. **Parent voices / testimonials** — short quotes/Reels (with consent). Doubles as review-generation fuel.
5. **Behind the team** — trained staff, certifications, safety protocols, hygiene. Builds the trust parents need to hand over a child.
6. **Community & events** — festivals (inclusive celebrations), awareness days, parent workshops, admission-open announcements.

### Posting cadence (sustainable)

- IG: 3–4 posts/week (≥2 Reels) + daily Stories. FB: cross-post 2–3×/week. WhatsApp Status: 2–3×/week. Always tag location + use 5–10 local + niche hashtags (`#inclusiveeducation #specialneedsindia #delhipreschool #newashoknagar #eastdelhimoms #earlyintervention`).
- **Consistency beats volume.** A steady cadence + responding to every comment/DM signals an active business and feeds brand-search volume (a real ranking input).

## Guardrails (carry through everything)

- **One canonical domain, identical NAP, everywhere.** This is the foundation; everything else amplifies it.
- **Reviews: earn them, never buy/incentivize; aim for steady velocity, not spikes.**
- **Backlinks: relevant + local + branded anchors.** Avoid link farms, article-directory spam, exact-match anchor over-optimization, and unrelated reciprocal-link schemes (toxic patterns).
- **Privacy:** never reveal a child's clinical/personal details in any public review reply, post, or profile. Always get parent consent before posting a child's photo.
- **Track everything** in the Citation Tracker so the off-page profile stays auditable and fixable.
